The premise

  • Training is a one-time event. Inference is forever.

  • Prompting, Reasoning, agentic systems, and privacy-sensitive workloads all live in the inference path.

Analysts project inference will claim ~75% of total AI compute by 2030. (Introl, 2025)

Test-time compute Capability

  • o3 / R1-style models generate 10–100× more tokens per query to reason, self-correct, and search
  • DeepSeek-R1 matched OpenAI o1 at ~70% lower cost - an inference engineering result, not a training one

Economics Savings

source

Control

  • Latency SLAs - TTFT and inter-token latency you can control
  • Determinism & reproducibility
  • Structured / constrained decoding
  • Custom sampling & logit control - your decoding strategy, your stop conditions, your guided generation
  • No rate limits, no deprecation notices, no vendor lock-in

Engineer the reliability and determinism, no dependency on vendor promises.

Agents multiply every inefficiency

  • Prompting is a single call. An agent is hundreds of calls - tool loops, re-planning, multi-turn context, parallel branches.
  • Reasoning + agentic workloads are throughput- and latency-bound, not capability-bound

Agent specific optimizations:

  • prefix caching
  • Scheduling
  • KV-cache management
  • Tool calling

etc

Privacy & data sovereignty

  • For some workloads, data cannot leave the org or national boundary.

  • Data protection acts - DPDP, GDPR, HIPAA etc

  • Healthcare, finance, defense, legal: prompts are the sensitive data

On-prem inference:

  • Data never crosses an org or national boundary
  • Auditable, immutable processing records
  • No third-party retention or training-on-your-data risk

Rapid advances in open-source LLMs

  • Customization and Fine-Tuning
  • Lower Total Cost of Ownership
  • No vendor lock-in
  • Running on Edge and on-premise
  • Models : Qwen, Gemma, LLama etc SLMs
  • Tooling : llama.cpp, vLLM, SGLang etc
